🎯 Project Aim

What HHC is built to achieve

🔍

Easy Health Discovery

Allow patients to quickly find verified doctors, hospitals, labs, and blood banks in their city without confusion or wasted time.

📋

Centralized Health Information

Bring all healthcare-related information — timings, specializations, bed availability, facilities — into one organized platform.

⭐

Transparent Reviews & Ratings

Let real patients share their experiences so others can make informed decisions when choosing a doctor or hospital.

🩸

Blood Bank Awareness

Help people in emergencies locate the nearest blood bank instantly and connect them with life-saving resources.

🏥

Digital Presence for Healthcare

Give hospitals, clinics, and labs a digital identity so they can be found online easily by the public.

📱

Accessible to Everyone

Provide a simple, mobile-friendly experience so anyone — regardless of tech knowledge — can use this platform with ease.

HHC creates value for every stakeholder in the healthcare ecosystem

🏥

For Hospitals

Grow your reach and manage your online presence

  • ✓ Free Digital Presence — Hospital ka naam, address, phone aur facilities online visible hoti hain
  • ✓ Doctor Directory — Hospital mein kaam karne wale tamam doctors listed hote hain patients k liye
  • ✓ Bed Availability Display — Total, ICU, General aur Private beds ki real-time info show ho sakti hai
  • ✓ Facility Showcase — Emergency, OT, Pharmacy, ICU jesi facilities ka status publicly display hota hai
  • ✓ Patient Reviews — Positive reviews se hospital ki credibility aur trust build hoti hai
  • ✓ More Patient Walk-ins — Online visibility se new patients discover karke aate hain
  • ✓ Admin Control — Admin panel se apni info khud update kar saktay hain

For Doctors

Build your professional profile and attract patients

  • ✓ Professional Profile — Name, photo, specialization aur qualification publicly listed hoti hai
  • ✓ Multi-Hospital Listing — Ek doctor multiple hospitals mein listed ho sakta hai apni har shift k saath
  • ✓ Clinic Timing Display — Morning/Evening shifts, working days aur off days sab clearly show hote hain

  • ✓ Patient Reviews — Real feedback se doctor ki online reputation strong hoti hai

🎬 Vlog Script

2-Minute Pitch Script

Hospitals, Doctors aur Labs ko convince karne ke liye — Urdu/Hindi

⏱ 0:00 – 0:15 · INTRO

"Assalam o Alaikum! Dosto, aaj main aapko ek aisi website ke baare mein batane wala hoon jo Pakistan mein healthcare ko completely change karne ki potential rakhti hai — aur iska naam hai HHC — Healthcare Hub."

⏱ 0:15 – 0:30 · PROBLEM

"Sochiye — ek mareez raat ko beemar parta hai. Use nahi pata kaunsa doctor available hai, hospital mein bed hai ya nahi, nazdeeqi lab kahan hai, ya blood bank ka number kya hai. Yeh confusion dangerous hai. Aur yahi problem HHC solve karti hai."

⏱ 0:30 – 0:50 · SOLUTION

"HHC ek free online directory hai jahan aap doctors, hospitals, labs aur blood banks — sab ek jagah dhundh saktay hain. City ke hisaab se filter karein, doctor ki timing check karein, hospital ki facilities dekhein, aur patient reviews padh kar bilkul confident decision lein."

⏱ 0:50 – 1:05 · HOSPITALS KE LIYE 🏥 Camera: Hospital admin ki taraf dekho

"Hospital administrators — aapka hospital HHC par list hoga toh aapki beds availability, ICU status, facilities aur doctors — sab online show honge. Naye patients khud aapko dhundh kar aayenge. Koi advertisement cost nahi, koi jhanjhat nahi — sirf apni info dein, baaki hum karein."

⏱ 1:05 – 1:20 · DOCTORS KE LIYE 👨‍⚕️ Camera: Doctor ki taraf dekho

"Doctors — aapki profile, aapki specialization, aapke clinic ki morning aur evening timings, multiple hospitals — sab ek jagah listed. Patients aapko pehle online dekhenge, timings check karenge, aur prepared hokar aayenge. Aapka time waste nahi hoga, aur aapki reputation online build hoti rahegi."

⏱ 1:20 – 1:35 · LABS KE LIYE 🔬 Camera: Lab assistant ki taraf dekho

"Lab assistants aur lab owners — aapki lab ka address, contact number aur city — HHC par free mein listed hogi. Jab bhi koi patient apne area mein lab dhundhe ga, aapka naam sabse pehle aayega. Reviews ke zariye aapki lab ki trust value bhi barh ti rahegi."

⏱ 1:35 – 2:00 · CALL TO ACTION

"Toh agar aap ek hospital admin hain, doctor hain, ya lab chalate hain — aaj hi apni information hamein send karein. Bilkul free hai. Sirf ek WhatsApp message karein:
