💡 SehatNexa — Kiske Liye Hai?

Har stakeholder ke liye alag faida — patients se leke healthcare providers tak

🌙

Sochiye raat ka waqt hai, ghar mein achanak kisi ko dil ki takleef ho jati hai. Ghar walay pareshan hain — samajh nahi aa raha ke kis hospital se rabta karein, kis doctor ke paas jayen, timing ka pata nahi, laboratory kahan hogi, aur agar blood ki zaroorat par jaye toh blood bank ka contact number kya hoga?

In sare problems ko hal karne ke liye aa raha hai — SehatNexa.
Ek online healthcare directory jahan patients apne shehar ke hisaab se hospitals, doctors, labs aur blood banks ki info ek hi jagah hasel kar sakenge.
