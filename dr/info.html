Indexing Check Karein Aapki website par jo doctors, labs, aur hospitals database mein hain, Google unhein khud dhoond lega. Lekin aapko Search Console mein "URL Inspection" tool se check karna hoga ke aapke pages Google mein index ho rahe hain ya nahi. 3. Content (Articles/Guides) Google sirf directory ko rank nahi karta (wo usay "thin content" samajhta hai). Aapko apni website par Blog ya Health Guides likhni hongi. Misaal: "Blood Bank kaise dhoondhein?", "Karachi mein best Cardiologist kaise chunein?", "Lab test se pehle kya parheez karein?". Yeh articles aapke listing pages ko power denge. 4. Reviews & Backlinks Jab log aapki website par reviews denge, to aapki site ki trust value barhegi. Doctors aur hospitals ko kahein ke wo apni website se aapki directory (SehatNexa) ka link dein.
